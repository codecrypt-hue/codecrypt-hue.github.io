How It Works

LV1

Get a Theme

Every team is handed a trendy, unconventional theme at the start — no picking, no planning ahead. That's the fun part.

LV2

Choose Your Problem Statement

Based on your theme, lock in a problem statement that your team wants to tackle within the time limit.

LV3

Brainstorm · Build · Present

Race against the clock to ideate, build, and present — all within the 3-hour window. No intense rules, just momentum.
