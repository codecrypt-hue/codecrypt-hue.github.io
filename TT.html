"Draw · Guess · Win" — Tech Pictionary

Teams guess technical terms based on drawings created by their teammates within a limited time. Words include programming languages, computer hardware, software concepts, networking, AI, cybersecurity, famous technologies, and Independence Day-themed technical concepts. The activity promotes quick thinking, teamwork, technical vocabulary, and communication skills.
